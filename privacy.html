Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Paisa Planner is a budgeting app for you and your family. This page explains what it stores, why, and your choices. It's written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, Gmail address and profile photo. We never get access to your Gmail inbox or any other Google data.
  • What you enter: budgets, categories, expenses and income, notes, bills and EMIs, loans, credit cards (name, limit, statement amounts), bank accounts (bank name, type and only the last 4 digits, never the full number), savings goals, investments you choose to add, and reminder times.
  • For reminders: a notification address for each phone where you turn reminders on.

We don't collect your location, contacts, SMS messages or bank passwords. If you share a message to the app, it's read on your phone to fill in an entry and isn't stored as-is.

Why we use it

  • To show your budget, balances and reminders, and to share one budget with the family members you invite.
  • Nothing else. We don't sell data, show ads, or share it with advertisers.

Who can see it

  • Only the members of your family budget, according to their role (Owner, Admin, Member or Viewer). The Owner can hide income and net worth from Members and Viewers.
  • The person who runs this copy of the app manages the database it uses (Supabase, stored in Mumbai, India) and can technically access it for maintenance.

Where it's stored

In a Supabase database hosted in India, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). If family sync isn't set up, data stays only on your device.

Your choices

  • Export your data any time (Settings → Export).
  • Leave a family (Family tab) or delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account). Deletion removes your sign-in, profile and reminders straight away; entries you added stay with the family's budget under your name.
  • The Owner can delete the whole family budget and all its data from Settings.
  • Turn reminders off any time in Settings, or in your phone's notification settings.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: write to the person who invited you, or to the app's owner at the email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
